Share Emergency Profile

Clinicians tap your phone or scan QR to access your data instantly — even if you can't speak

1
Get an NFC sticker
An NTAG215 sticker (£2–5) sticks inside your phone case. Works even when phone battery is dead.
2
Tap to write your token
We write an encrypted, tokenised URL — not your actual data. Only verified clinicians can resolve it.
3
Any NFC reader can tap it
Clinicians tap their phone on your sticker. They're prompted to log in to see your profile. Access is audited.
